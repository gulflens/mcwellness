# Your information

McWellness, September 2026. Half a page on what we keep and why.

**What we keep — your ordinary details.** Your name and contact details, your
date of birth, the address we visit, the agreements you sign, your
appointments, and what you have paid. For a child, the parent's or guardian's
name and contact details too. If you give us your Emirates ID number we store
it sealed so staff cannot read it back; we never keep a picture of an ID.

**What we keep — your health information.** Your session records: the sensor
readings, any brain map (qEEG) and the reports written from it, the health
answers you gave us, and the ratings you give before and after a session.
This is more sensitive than the rest, so we ask for it separately and you
agree to it separately, on the page "Your brain-map and neurofeedback
information".

**Why.** To plan and deliver your sessions safely, to make each session
better than the last, to contact you about appointments, and to issue the
invoices and receipts the law requires.

**Who sees it.** Your practitioner, the practice's staff where their job
needs it, and the technology provider that hosts our system, under contract
and only to run it.

**How we will not use it.** We never sell your information. We never use it
for advertising. We never use it for research. We never use it for anything
unrelated to your sessions and your account with us. If that ever changes we
will ask you first, separately, and you may say no.

**Where it is kept.** Our system is hosted in Mumbai, India, by our hosting
provider.

**How long.** Five years after your last session or contact with us, then it
is deleted. Invoices and receipts are kept as long as tax law requires.

**Your choices.** Ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your
information, or to withdraw an agreement. Deleting removes your personal
details; invoices, and a note that a deletion happened, are kept without
them.

**Confidentiality.** What you tell us stays in the practice, unless the law
requires us to share it or we believe someone is in serious danger.
