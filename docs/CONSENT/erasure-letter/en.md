# Confirmation that your record has been erased

**Draft wording, in use until the practice's lawyer approves a final version.**

On {{erased_on}} we erased the record you asked us to erase. Your name,
your date of birth, the contact details we held, the address we visited and
the notes and files kept with them are gone from our systems, and the account
that opened the client portal has been closed. What remains where a record
used to be is an entry saying that a record was erased on that date, holding
none of your personal details.

Two things are kept, and only because the law requires them. Invoices and
receipts stay for five years, as UAE tax law requires of every business; they
carry what a tax invoice must carry and nothing further about you. The
practice also keeps a log of what was done and when, for its own five years,
and the entries written by this erasure record the names of the fields that
were cleared without any of the values that were in them. Nothing else about
you is held, and nobody at {{practice_legal_name}} can bring back what has
gone. If you have a question about this, reply to the message this letter came
with, or contact us at [the practice's contact details]. You may also complain
to the UAE Data Office.
