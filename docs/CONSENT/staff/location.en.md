# Sharing your location while you work

McWellness, Dubai. October 2026. The practice's notice to the people who visit
households for it. Read it before you turn sharing on. It is your choice, and
you can change your mind at any time.

## Why we ask

So whoever is coordinating the day can see where you are on the office's map
while you are working: to fit a change to the day around where you actually
are, to tell a household honestly when you will arrive, and to know that
somebody driving alone between houses is where they should be. That is the
whole purpose.

## What is collected

Your phone's position, from your phone's own location service: where you are,
when, and how sure the phone is of it. Nothing else — not your speed, not your
route, not anything else on your phone.

## When

Only when all three of these are true:

- you have agreed to this page, in your own app;
- your switch, "Share my location while I work", is on;
- you are working. Your working day is read from your own visits: it starts an
  hour and a half before your first visit's arrival window, and ends half an
  hour after your last visit is finished — or after it could at the latest
  have finished, if it was never closed. It never runs past 9 in the evening,
  even if a visit was left open, and on a day with no visits there is no
  working day at all.

Because the working day begins before your first visit, the first position of
the day can be your home, or wherever you set off from.

And only while the app is open on your phone. The app does not follow you in
the background. Whenever sharing is on, the app shows a band at the top of
your day saying so, and the date you agreed, so it is never quietly running.

## Who sees it

The owner, the practice's administrator and the lead practitioner — the people
who coordinate the day — on the office's board and map. They see your last
position and how long ago it was sent, and only while your working day is
open; never a trail of where you have been. Other practitioners do not see it.
Households never see it. Finance never sees it.

The office's map is drawn by Google Maps. Your position is drawn on it by the
practice's own app and is sent in no request to Google; Google is sent only the
part of the map being looked at, as with any map, and never your name.

## How long it is kept

Two days, and then it is deleted, automatically, every hour. Only your last
position is ever shown; nobody can look back through the earlier ones, which
are simply waiting to be deleted. Positions are never copied into the
practice's audit record, and the practice's own weekly backup leaves positions
out.

The company that hosts the practice's database also keeps its own daily copies
of the whole system, for its own backup period, so that the system can be
restored after a fault. A position can sit in those copies until they age out.
They are not read for anything else.

The practice's audit record does keep, for at least five years, when you
agreed or withdrew, each time you turned sharing on or off, and who looked at
the board. It is the record of your choices and of who saw your location.
Those times could show when you were working; like your position, they are
never used for your pay, your hours or how well you are doing your job.

## What it is never used for

Your pay. Your hours. How well you are doing your job. Any decision about you
at all. It is for coordinating the day, and nothing else. If that ever changes
we will ask you first, separately, and you may say no.

## How to stop

Turn the switch off in your app. Sharing stops at once, and the board shows
"Not sharing now" rather than your last position — as it also does once your
working day has closed.

Or withdraw this agreement, also in your app. Sharing stops at once, and every
position of yours still held is deleted there and then. You can always do
this, even if your role at the practice has changed. Saying no, or stopping,
costs you nothing and changes nothing about your work.

You can also ask the practice what it holds about you, and ask for it to be
corrected or deleted, at any time: ask the practice owner, in person or
through the app, or write to the practice's email address, which the owner
keeps in the app under Settings › Practice.

## Agreement

Pressing "I agree, share my location" in your own app is your written agreement
to sharing your location as this page describes. Nobody else can press it for
you, and nobody — the owner included — can turn sharing on for you. The owner
can reset your password, so could in principle sign in as you; a password
reset is written in the practice's audit record, and the date you agreed is
always on your own screen, so an agreement you did not give would show. The practice approved this notice on 6 October
2026, and that approval stands as its signature.
