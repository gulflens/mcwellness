# Agreement for a child or young person to take part

**Draft wording, in use until the practice's lawyer approves a final version.**

This agreement is signed by a parent or legal guardian on behalf of a client
who is under eighteen. It is given together with the *Agreement to take part
in McWellness sessions*, which describes the services, the possible effects
and how personal information is handled, and which applies in full to the
child.

## 1. Who may sign

A parent, or a person holding legal guardianship, who has the authority to
make decisions about the child's wellbeing. [The lawyer confirms who may
consent for a minor under UAE law, and whether the practice must see a
document.] If the child's other parent or guardian shares that authority,
the person signing confirms that they are entitled to decide, or that the
other has agreed.

## 2. The child's own agreement

We explain to the child, in words suited to their age, what a session
involves and that they can stop at any time. We do not continue with a
child who is distressed or refuses, whatever this agreement says. A child's
"no" ends the session.

## 3. Being present

A parent, guardian or an adult the guardian has named is present in the
home, and within call, throughout every session with a child under [age].
[The lawyer and the practice confirm the age below which presence in the
room, rather than the home, is required.]

## 4. Information about the child

Everything in section 6 of the participation agreement applies to the
child's information, with these additions:

- We share the child's record with the parents or guardians named on the
  record, and with nobody else without a separate, written permission,
  including the child's school, other professionals, or a parent not named
  on the record.
- Where the parents disagree about the child taking part, we pause until
  they agree or the matter is decided elsewhere.
- The child may, at any age, tell us something they ask us to keep from
  their parents. We keep that confidence unless the child or someone else
  is at serious risk, and we tell the child when we cannot.

## 5. Health information about the child

Please tell us, before the first session and whenever anything changes, if
the child has epilepsy or has ever had a seizure, has any implanted
electrical device, has had a head injury at any time, takes medication
that affects mood, sleep, attention or the nervous system, or has a skin
condition or sensitivity on the scalp. This is for the practitioner's
planning, not a medical assessment.

## 6. Withdrawing

The parent or guardian who signed, or any other person with the same
authority, may withdraw this agreement at any time by telling the practice.
The child may also stop at any time. Future sessions are cancelled; a
session in progress finishes normally.

## 7. Agreement

By signing, I confirm that:

- I am the parent or legal guardian of the child named below, and I have
  the authority to make this decision.
- I have read the *Agreement to take part in McWellness sessions* and this
  document, or had them read to me, in a language I understand, and I have
  had the chance to ask questions.
- I agree to the child taking part in McWellness sessions as described,
  and to McWellness holding and using the child's personal information as
  described.
- I understand that the child can stop at any time, and so can I.
