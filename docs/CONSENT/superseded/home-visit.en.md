# Agreement to sessions at home

**Draft wording, in use until the practice's lawyer approves a final version.**

This agreement is given by the client, or by a parent or guardian for a
client under eighteen, when sessions take place at the client's home or
another private address. It adds to the *Agreement to take part in
McWellness sessions*.

## 1. Coming to your home

- A practitioner comes to the address you give us, within the arrival
  window we agree, and lets you know when they are on their way.
- You, or an adult you name, let the practitioner in. We do not enter a
  home where nobody expected us.
- For a client under eighteen, a parent, guardian or an adult the guardian
  has named is at home throughout the session.

## 2. A safe place for a session

Please provide a quiet space with a chair or sofa, a small table and a
power socket, and keep pets and other children away from the equipment
during the session. The practitioner may decline to start, or may stop, if
the space is not safe for the client, the practitioner or the equipment,
and will tell you why.

## 3. Your address and how to find you

To deliver visits we keep your address, the location pin, the parking
place, the gate or entrance, and any directions you give us, and we share
them with the practitioner assigned to you. When the practitioner sets off,
their app opens a map at your entrance's coordinates so they can find you;
only the coordinates are sent to the map service, never your name or any
other detail.

## 4. Proof of the visit

When the practitioner arrives, they check in on their phone. If they choose
to, the check-in records where they were at that moment, as proof that the
visit took place. This is kept with the session record, seen only by the
practice, and never used to track anyone.

## 5. What the practitioner will and will not do

The practitioner works only in the room agreed for the session, brings and
takes away their own equipment, does not photograph the home except under
the separate photo agreement, and does not give or accept gifts or private
arrangements. If anything about a visit concerns you, tell the practice.

## 6. Changes and cancellations

The notice we ask for, and the fee for a late cancellation or a visit that
cannot go ahead when the practitioner arrives, are in the practice's current
terms. [The lawyer confirms the amounts and periods.]

## 7. Agreement

By signing, I confirm that:

- I am entitled to invite the practitioner into the address given.
- I have read this document, or had it read to me, in a language I
  understand.
- I agree to sessions taking place at the address given, as described.
