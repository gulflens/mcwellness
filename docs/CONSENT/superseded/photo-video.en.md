# Agreement to photographs during sessions

**Draft wording, in use until the practice's lawyer approves a final version.**

This agreement is optional. Sessions go ahead without it. It is given by the
client, or by a parent or guardian for a client under eighteen, and it adds
to the *Agreement to take part in McWellness sessions*.

## 1. What we photograph, and why

With this agreement the practitioner may take a photograph of the sensor
placement on the scalp at the start of a session, so that the next session
can repeat it exactly and so the record shows how the session was set up.
Where possible the photograph shows the sensors and not the face.

We do not record video, and we do not photograph the home, the family or
anything else.

## 2. Where the photographs go

Each photograph is stored with the session record, seen only by the
practitioner and the practice's staff whose job needs it, and kept for the
same period as the record: five years after your last session or contact
with us, then deleted. We never use these photographs for advertising,
social media, teaching or any purpose outside your own record, and we never
share them outside the practice without a separate, written permission.

## 3. Withdrawing

You may withdraw this agreement at any time by telling your practitioner
or the practice. No further photographs are taken, and the ones already
taken are deleted from your record unless one is part of a report already
issued to you, in which case we tell you.

## 4. Agreement

By signing, I confirm that:

- I have read this document, or had it read to me, in a language I
  understand.
- I agree to photographs of the sensor placement being taken and kept as
  described.
- I understand that this is optional and that I can withdraw at any time.
