# Offers from McWellness on your phone

McWellness, Dubai. October 2026. This page asks whether you would like us to
send you our offers. It is separate from everything else you have agreed to,
because it is the one thing we would do with your details that is not about
your sessions. Saying no is as easy as saying yes, and changes nothing.

## What we would send

Offers: a discount, a package price, a seasonal offer. Every discount we
mention is one on our own price list, so the offer and your invoice always say
the same thing. We never make a medical claim, in any language, in anything we
send.

Practice news — the studio closed for a holiday, a new practitioner — is not
an offer. It appears on your portal home for every adult of every household,
whether or not you turn this on.

## How often

At most two offers a month. Often fewer.

## How it reaches you

As a notification on your phone, from your McWellness portal, once the portal
has been added to your phone's home screen. Not by WhatsApp, not by text
message, not by email.

The phone's own notification service (Apple's, Google's or Mozilla's,
depending on the phone) carries the message to you. It is sealed before it
leaves us: that service learns which device to deliver to, and never your name,
your number or a word of the message.

## Who it is for

You, an adult, on your own portal sign-in, and nobody else on your record. We
never send an offer to a young person's own sign-in.

## What we use

That you are a client of ours, and the device address your phone gives the
portal when you turn this on. Nothing from your sessions, your measurements,
your health answers or your reports, ever.

## Stopping

One press. Turn the switch off on your portal, under Agreements, or use the
"stop" in any offer, and nothing more is sent from that moment. You can turn it
on again whenever you like.

## What does not change

Your sessions, your prices and how we look after you are the same whether you say
yes, no, or nothing at all. We still do not sell your information, and we still
do not use it for research.

## What we keep

A record of every offer we send: what it said, when, who sent it, how many
people received it, and whether your switch was on at that moment. So we can
show you, or anyone entitled to ask, exactly what we did.

## Turning this on

I would like McWellness to send me its offers, at most two a month, as
notifications on my phone from my portal, as this page describes. I know I can
stop them with one press at any time.
