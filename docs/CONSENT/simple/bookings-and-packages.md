# Bookings and packages

McWellness, September 2026. How the practical side works.

**Moving or cancelling a visit.** Tell us at least 24 hours before. With
less notice, one session from your package is used. If a visit cannot go
ahead once the practitioner has arrived, the same applies.

**Arrival.** We give you an arrival window rather than a fixed minute,
because visits are in people's homes across the city. If we are running
late we tell you.

**Packages.** Sessions in a package are valid for twelve months from the
date of purchase. Your balance and the sessions remaining are on every
receipt, and in the app when it is ready.

**Stopping early.** If you stop before a package is used up, the unused
sessions are refunded at the price you paid per session.

**Paying.** Cash, bank transfer or a payment link, with a receipt for each
payment. Prices are shown on the price list; VAT is added only if the
practice is registered for it, and the invoice says which.

**Questions.** Ask your practitioner, or message the practice on WhatsApp.
