# Agreement to take part in McWellness sessions

**Draft wording, in use until the practice's lawyer approves a final version.**

## 1. Who we are, and what this is

McWellness ([legal name], trade licence [number], tax registration number
[TRN], [registered address]) is a wellness practice. We offer neurofeedback
training, brain-mapping assessment (qEEG), Compassionate Inquiry sessions and
consultations, delivered at your home or another place we agree with you.

This document explains what taking part involves, how we look after your
personal information, and asks for your agreement. Please read it, ask us
anything that is unclear, and keep a copy.

## 2. What our services are, and what they are not

- Neurofeedback is a form of training. Small sensors placed on the scalp
  measure the brain's natural electrical activity, and you watch or listen
  to feedback that responds to it. Nothing is put into the body, and no
  current is applied.
- A brain map (qEEG) is a measurement recorded the same way. It shows
  patterns of activity. It is not a diagnosis.
- McWellness is a wellness provider, not a medical clinic. We do not
  diagnose or treat medical or psychiatric conditions, and we do not
  prescribe or advise on medication. Our services are not a substitute for
  care from your doctor or other health professional, and you should keep
  seeing them.
- We are not an emergency service. If you or someone with you is in
  danger, call 998 (ambulance) or 999 (police).
- People respond differently. We cannot promise a particular result.

## 3. What a session involves

- The practitioner places sensors on your scalp and ears with a small amount
  of paste or gel, which washes out with water.
- You sit comfortably and watch a screen or listen to sound for a set time.
  Sessions usually last between thirty and sixty minutes.
- A practitioner is present throughout. You may pause or stop at any time,
  for any reason, without giving one.
- A programme is a series of sessions. The number, the spacing and the
  place are agreed with you and written in your plan.

## 4. Possible effects

Most people notice nothing unwelcome. Some people notice, for a few hours
and occasionally a day, tiredness, a mild headache, light-headedness,
irritability, vivid dreams or a change in sleep. These usually pass without
doing anything. Tell the practitioner if you notice anything, during or
after a session, so the next one can be adjusted. If you feel unwell, stop.

So that the practitioner can adapt to you safely, please tell us before your
first session, and whenever anything changes, if you:

- have epilepsy, or have ever had a seizure;
- have a pacemaker or any implanted electrical device;
- have had a head injury at any time;
- are pregnant;
- take medication that affects mood, sleep, attention or the nervous
  system;
- have a skin condition, wound or sensitivity on the scalp.

This is information for the practitioner, not a medical assessment. If you
are unsure whether something matters, tell us and we will ask you to check
with your doctor.

## 5. What we ask of you

- Give us accurate information, and tell us when it changes.
- Come to sessions rested, having eaten, and without alcohol or recreational
  substances in the hours before.
- Treat the practitioner with respect. We may end a session, or a
  programme, if a practitioner is not safe or is treated badly.

## 6. Your personal information

**What we hold.** Your name and contact details; date of birth; the address
where we visit you, with directions and access notes; the record of your
sessions, including the measurements from the sensors, the ratings you give
before and after, and the practitioner's observations; the consents you
give; and the record of what you have paid. If you choose to give us your
Emirates ID number, we store it in sealed form that our staff cannot read;
we never keep an image of an identity document.

**Why.** To plan and deliver your sessions safely, to keep the record that
makes the next session better than the last, to contact you about your
appointments, and to issue the invoices and receipts that UAE tax law
requires.

**On what basis.** Your agreement in this document, and the contract between
us for the services you buy.

**Who sees it.** The practitioner who works with you; the practice's staff,
each only as much as their job needs; and the technology providers who host
our systems, under contract and only to run those systems. We will give you
the list of those providers on request. We do not sell your information and
we do not use it for advertising.

**Where it is kept.** Our systems are hosted by a provider whose servers are
[in the hosting region, currently outside the UAE]. [The lawyer confirms the
lawful basis for this transfer under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law
and the wording to use here.]

**How long.** Five years after your last session or contact with us, then
deleted. Invoices and receipts are kept for the period tax law requires.

**Your rights.** You may ask to see the information we hold about you, have
it corrected, or have it deleted; you may withdraw any consent at any time;
and you may complain to the UAE Data Office. Deletion removes your personal
details from our records; invoices, and the record that a deletion took
place, are kept as the law requires, without your personal details.

**Confidentiality.** What you tell us stays within the practice. The
exceptions are where the law requires us to share it, or where we believe
someone is at serious risk of harm.

## 7. Appointments, changes and payment

Your appointments, how to change or cancel them, the notice we ask for, the
fee for a late cancellation or a visit that cannot go ahead, the period a
package of sessions stays valid, and how refunds work are set out in the
practice's current terms, which we give you with this document. [The lawyer
confirms the amounts and periods, and where they are shown.]

## 8. Withdrawing

You may withdraw this agreement at any time by telling your practitioner or
the practice. Future sessions are cancelled. A session already in progress
finishes normally. Anything you have paid for sessions not yet delivered is
handled under the practice's terms.

- I agree to take part in McWellness sessions as described.
- I agree to McWellness holding and using my personal information as
  described in section 6.
- I understand that I can stop at any time.
