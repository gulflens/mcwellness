# ADR 0003 — A wellness business on Supabase Cloud
Decision (2026-09-02): McWellness is a wellness company, not a licensed clinic (founder's determination). No DHA licence, no NABIDH, no 25-year retention. Production runs on Supabase Cloud in the region the owner chooses; the self-hosted AWS UAE plan, Terraform and the region guard are dropped. Retention is 5 years after the last activity, then erasure or anonymisation on request. Every service is standard-rated for VAT. The product records goals, sessions and measurements, never diagnoses or treatments.
Why: the strictest rules in the brief were consequences of the clinic classification. Privacy-first remains a product choice: consent at execution, the audit trail, structured records and the vendor register stay. Hosting outside the UAE is to be confirmed with the founder's lawyer under the UAE personal-data law.
